Partnerships

Rural transformation requires many capabilities.

BIRD is designed to orchestrate finance, markets, technology, knowledge, infrastructure and policy around measurable outcomes.

Partnership Architecture

One mission. Multiple capabilities. Shared impact.

BIRD does not need to perform every rural development function itself. Its value can come from creating common frameworks, financing viable activities and coordinating partners around measurable outcomes.

🏛️

Government & Regulators

Policy, infrastructure, public data, enabling frameworks and targeted programmes.

🏦

Banks, DFIs & Investors

Capital, liquidity, payments, risk sharing, co-financing and institutional expertise.

🏭

Private Sector & Value Chains

Inputs, processing, logistics, buyers, exporters, technology and investment.

🌍

Development Partners

Technical assistance, catalytic capital, guarantees, innovation and global learning.

🎓

Research & Academia

Evidence, evaluation, innovation, training, capacity building and policy learning.

🤝

Rural Communities

Local priorities, participation, trust, accountability and community ownership.

Ways to Partner

From capital to capability.

Finance

Investment, co-financing, liquidity, guarantees and risk-sharing structures.

Markets

Procurement, processing, offtake, warehousing, logistics, trade and exports.

Technology

Digital onboarding, data, platforms, payments, analytics and responsible AI.

Knowledge

Training, advisory, research, monitoring, evaluation and institutional learning.
