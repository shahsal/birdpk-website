RTI & Impact

Measure what changes after finance.

Rural Transformation Index

RTI is proposed as a live management system for measuring whether BIRD is producing durable rural economic change.

Human Capability

Financial literacy, technical skills, advisory participation and digital literacy.

Productive Capacity

Productivity, productive assets, technology adoption and income diversification.

Enterprise Strength

Revenue, investment, formalization and working-capital stability.

Financial Inclusion

Active accounts, savings, finance access and formal participation.

Market Access

Buyer linkages, transaction volume, market diversification and value addition.

Resilience & Sustainability

Climate risk management, recovery capacity, infrastructure and environmental performance.
